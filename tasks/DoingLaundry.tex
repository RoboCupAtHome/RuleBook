\section{Doing Laundry Challenge}\label{test:doing-laundry}
The robot assists with laundry by retrieving clothes from a washing machine and folding them neatly on a table. This test evaluates deformable object manipulation, appliance interaction, and task sequencing.\\

\noindent \textbf{Main goal:} Fold clothes.\\

\noindent \textbf{Optional goals:}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
    \item Opening the washing machine door
    \item Retrieving clothes from inside the washing machine
    \item Place clothing on the table.
    \item Using a laundry basket for transportation
    \item Folding multiple pieces of clothing and stacking them
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Focus}
\emph{Deformable object manipulation}, \emph{bimanual grasping}.

% %% %%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
% Setup
% %% %%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection*{Setup}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Locations:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item \textbf{Start location:} Before the test, the robot waits outside the \Arena{} and navigates to the laundry area when the door is open.
            \item \textbf{Test locations:} The testing area contains a washing machine, two folding surfaces, and a laundry basket.
        \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{People:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item No people are involved in the test, unless the robot requires human assistance.
        \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Furniture:}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item \textbf{Laundry basket:} A lightweight basket requiring two arms to carry, placed near the washing machine. The basket already has 6--8 pieces of clothing placed inside.
            \item \textbf{Washing machine:} Positioned with its door closed at the start. Clothes are placed inside. There are 2--4 pieces of clothing in the washing machine.
            \item \textbf{Tables:} Positioned near the washing machine. Used as the folding surfaces. There is a single T-shirt placed flat on one of the tables.
        \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Objects}:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item \textbf{Clothes:} A set of 8--12 pieces of laundry made from cloth or other deformable fabric.
        \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

% %% %%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
% Procedure
% %% %%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection*{Procedure}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Location announcement:} At least two hours before the test, referees announce which washing machine and table will be used, along with the approximate location of each.
    \item \textbf{Test start:} The robot enters the testing area once the arena door is opened.
    \item \textbf{Shirt Folding:} The robot folds the shirt already placed on one of the tables.
    \item \textbf{Retrieving laundry:} The robot retrieves additional clothes from the laundry basket or directly from the washing machine and places them on the table.
    \item \textbf{More Folding:} The robot attempts to fold at least one piece of clothing neatly.
\end{enumerate}

% %% %%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
% Additional Rules
% %% %%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection*{Additional rules and remarks}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Laundry Types:} The laundry will consist of T-shirts and trousers. Other clothes may be announced on site.
    \item \textbf{Cloth placement:} Clothes must be placed on the table before folding. Folding on the floor is not allowed.
    \item \textbf{Folding quality:} Folding is evaluated by neatness and whether the cloth is flattened and stacked (see~\refapp{app:score-folding} for examples. Scores are assigned holistically based on the final folding quality after applying any penalties).
    \item \textbf{Multiple items:} Additional points are awarded for folding multiple pieces of clothing and stacking them.
    \item \textbf{Basket transportation:} Using the basket to transport clothing from the washing machine to the table. The basket must be placed somewhere on the folding surfaces or in reach of the folding surface such that the robot can reach the clothing quickly.
    \item \textbf{Human Assistance:} Scores are reduced if human assistance is received, in particular for:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item opening the washing machine door
        \item \textbf{Folding:} Penalties are proportional to the amount of help provided:
        \begin{itemize}[nosep]
            \item Minimal help (e.g., smoothing wrinkles, stabilizing cloth): small penalty (~100 points).
            \item Partial folding (e.g., folding one half or aligning edges): moderate penalty (~200 points).
            \item Major help (e.g., completing entire fold/stack): maximum penalty.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{OC Instructions}
At least two hours before the test:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Announce which washing machine and table will be used.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Referee Instructions}
The referee needs to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Place one of the T-shirts flat on a folding surface.
    \item Ensure the washing machine and basket contains the laundry before the test begins.
    \item Close the washing machine door at the start.
    \item Verify that the folding table is clear before the test.
\end{itemize}
